# Ledger ceiling observation

This is an independent sampled-ledger observation, not a before/after effect claim.

The largest observed summed block `costUnits` were:

- 85,358,991 against an 87,500,000 pre-boundary cap (97.55%).
- 72,807,739 against a 75,000,000 post-boundary cap (97.08%).

Each maximum is just below, not above, its applicable candidate cap. This is direct evidence from the sampled landed blocks that the two active cost-unit ceilings were reached closely enough to validate the utilization denominators. It survives transaction-mix, fee-market, and before/after-comparison confounders because it makes no claim about a change across the epoch boundary.
